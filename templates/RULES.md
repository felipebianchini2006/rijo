# Rules

- Artifacts over conversation: no important progress lives only in chat.
- No completion without objective evidence (commands, exit codes, tests, commit).
- Conservative hypothesis + DECISIONS.md entry instead of unnecessary questions.
- Bounded loops: 2 plan revisions, 2 review cycles, 2 QA-fix rounds, 2 quick-fix attempts, 4 parallel agents.
- Workers never write outside their declared scope.
- Historic milestones are immutable.
